Ginger-Soy Glazed Pork Chops

Pan-seared chops with a sticky ginger-garlic pan sauce, made for spooning over rice.

Prep5 min Cook13 min Total18 min Serves2

Ingredients

  • 2 pork chops, about 1 inch thick
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 1/2 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • Cooked rice, for serving

Instructions

  1. Prep the chops

    Pat the pork chops very dry and season both sides with the salt and black pepper. Stir together the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar and water in a small bowl.

  2. Sear

    Heat the oil in a heavy skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Sear the chops without moving them until deeply browned, about 4 minutes per side, then stand them on the fat edge for 30 seconds. Pull them at 135 to 140°F inside and move to a plate.

  3. Build the glaze

    Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic and ginger and stir for 30 seconds, then pour in the soy mixture and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer until syrupy, about 2 minutes.

  4. Finish

    Take the pan off the heat and swirl in the butter. Return the chops and any resting juices to the pan and turn to coat.

  5. Rest and serve

    Rest the chops for 3 minutes so they coast to 145°F. Slice, lay over rice, spoon on the glaze and top with the scallions.

Thin chops (under 3/4 inch) need only 2 to 3 minutes per side. No fresh ginger? Use 1/2 tsp ground, or swap in a spoonful of chili crisp.

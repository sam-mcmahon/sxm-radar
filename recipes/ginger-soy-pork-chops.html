Ginger-Soy Glazed Pork Chops

Seared on the stove, finished in a 400°F convection oven, then coated in a sticky ginger-garlic pan sauce.

Prep5 min Cook13 min Total18 min Serves2

Ingredients

  • 2 pork chops, about 1 inch thick
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 1/2 tbsp honey or brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 3 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • Cooked rice, for serving

Instructions

  1. Prep the chops

    Heat the oven to 400°F on convection roast. Pat the pork chops very dry and season both sides with the salt and black pepper. Stir together the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, water, garlic and ginger in a small bowl.

  2. Sear

    Heat the oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Sear the chops without moving them until deeply browned, about 2 minutes per side, then stand them on the fat edge for 30 seconds.

  3. Roast

    Move the skillet to the oven and roast for 4 to 6 minutes, until the chops reach 135 to 140°F inside. Move the chops to a plate to rest; they will coast to 145°F while you make the glaze.

  4. Build the glaze

    Set the skillet over medium heat, minding the hot handle. Pour in the soy mixture and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer for about 3 minutes, until syrupy and the garlic and ginger have cooked through.

  5. Finish and serve

    Take the pan off the heat and swirl in the butter. Return the chops and any resting juices to the pan and turn to coat. Slice, lay over rice, spoon on the glaze and top with the scallions.

Thin chops (under 3/4 inch) need only 2 to 3 minutes in the oven after the sear. No fresh ginger? Use 1/2 tsp ground, or swap in a spoonful of chili crisp.
